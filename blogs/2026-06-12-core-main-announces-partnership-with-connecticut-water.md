ST. LOUIS, June 12, 2026—Core & Main, Inc. (NYSE: CNM), a leading specialty distributor dedicated to advancing reliable infrastructure with local service, nationwide, today announced it has been selected by Connecticut Water Company (CWC) to provide an Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) system across its service area.
